Folks, please be careful with each other and with the topic of the thread. A couple notes:
  • Indigenous/First Nations people are welcome to express their feelings, and those feelings aren't automatically destructive to the thread. If you think something violates the guidelines, flag it, but it's not anyone's job to tone-police angry victims of racism.
  • While there certainly are commonalities between anti-Indigenous racism in Canada and anti-Black racism in the US (and other varieties of racism globally), please use care so as not to derail the thread away from the topic of anti-Indigenous racism in Canada.
