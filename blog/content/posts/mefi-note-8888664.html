So, nothing else deleted, but i do need to leave a slightly complicated note here. To be clear, "dual loyalty" is an antisemitic trope because it is often applied to Jewish people simply because they are Jewish. If an individual person (of any faith or ethnic group) openly avers a loyalty to another country, it is generally perfectly fine to say "they are openly loyal to that other country and i find that unacceptable". But then saying something like this—"The Internet says you’re not supposed to say dual loyalty because that’s antisemitic but what else is it?"—which draws attention directly to the idea of the antisemitic trope? That swings back toward "not really okay". The reason to be very careful with allegations of dual loyalty is because it is often actually antisemitic, not because other people will call you antisemitic.
