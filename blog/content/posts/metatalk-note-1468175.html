Just for the record, the full comment that was deleted read as follows:

I was busy with work today, so I had Claude live-blog the ground stop. It's still blogging, but the updates have slowed down.


There was no indication in the comment that the poster had any special expertise or was sharing Claude output for some reason that should be interesting to MetaFilter. It read to me (and to some other users; the comment was flagged) as "I asked Claude and here's what it said", which is precisely what we don't allow. It felt very much like an end-run around the "don't post LLM output" guideline.

Folks are correct that we don't, for instance, ban Projects posts that contain stuff made with "AI". And while you don't have to believe me, as a known "AI"-hater i bend over backwards to try to make sure that i moderate our pro-"AI" users and their posts fairly.

It's very possible that i made the wrong call here! But also, if the comment had contained even a couple more sentences about why the output was theoretically useful or interesting, i almost certainly would've made a different call!
