Hi, folks. Just to reiterate in an official capacity: we do in fact have policy on this. You should not use "AI" to write your posts or comments, and if you feel it's absolutely necessary to do so, you must label that content. (The relevant section of the Content Policy is here.)

Obviously we don't actually have a magic way to detect every use of "AI" for posts and comments on this site. We're on the honor system, and you can probably get away with it, at least some of the time. But choosing that path means that you are deliberately deceiving your fellow community members. It is disrespectful and gross; people are definitely going to be mad if and when they discover it, and you will fully deserve that anger.
