One deleted. Comments in Ask must address the Asker, not other commenters. If someone else gives an answer you think is badly wrong, the best way to handle that is by flagging it. If you think it's crucially important to add a caveat about someone else's answer in public (and sometimes it might be!) you can do that as part of your own answer, but your comment does still need to contain an answer.

Also note: In general we do discourage "have you tried 'AI'?"-type answers, for the same reason we discourage "have you tried Google?"-type answers, but in this case it's relevant enough to let it stay.
